# KerbalWildsVentures
Companion mod to Kerbal Wilds, adds Outer Wilds themed parts to KSP.

Kerbal Wilds, the solar system mod, can be found here: https://github.com/WhirligigGirl/KerbalOuterWilds/releases

Parts are built to Kerbal standards in the ReStock art style--i.e., they won't be held together with rubber bands and wooden planks. These will be "Lego-able," and designed to add a Hearthian flare to normal Kerbal rockets, rather than intended for building an exact replica of the Outer Wilds ship in KSP, although it will help make your Outer Wilds ship replicas a lot more authentic.

Prerequisites:
* Resurfaced/Technicolor: https://github.com/Tantares/Resurfaced/releases (Currently a hard dependency but this may change.)
	* Which itself requires Shabby: https://github.com/KSPModdingLibs/Shabby/releases
	* And highly recommends Deferred: https://github.com/LGhassen/Deferred/releases
	
Recommended:
* ReStock PBR: https://github.com/PorktoberRevolution/ReStockPBR

Currently includes:
* Wood and weathered "Toasted Marshmallow" color swatches for Technicolor.
* DomePod25 ("OWV-22 Spaceship Cockpit"), based on the golden-domed cockpit from the Spaceship in Outer Wilds. 
    * Currently no IVA/Internal view, reuses the Mk1-3 pod internals.
	
Planned:
* DomePod 125 and 187.
* 2.5m-1.875m or 1.875m-1.25m adapter with six built-in struts, resembling the Outer Wilds ship's side-mounted hulls.
* Five-way engine mount.
* Multiple engine/RCS-thruster hybrid parts for various use cases.
   * NTR based on the in-game ship reactor model.
   * Vacuum-optimized thruster
   * Launch-optimized engine
* Wooden planks and struts, just for fun.
* Team Color support for Technicolor.
